About Wavefall

Wavefall is a hero defense game with turn-based tactical combat for iPhone, iPad, and Mac. A rogue AI sends its robots through a portal in waves, and you hold the arena alone: choose where to stand, which rock to hide behind and when to shoot. Between rounds you mine resources and turn them into new weapons with the 3D printer. Wavefall plays fully offline, with no ads and no subscriptions.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which platforms does it support?

Wavefall runs natively on iPhone, iPad, and Mac.

Is there a way to reset my progress?

Yes — Wavefall stores all progress locally on your device. If you'd like to start over, email us at {{ email }} and we'll walk you through it.

I found a bug — what should I include in my report?

Please include your device model, iOS/iPadOS/macOS version, what you were doing when the issue occurred, and a screenshot or screen recording if possible. This helps us reproduce and fix the issue faster.

Does Wavefall require an internet connection or an account?

No. Wavefall is fully offline and single-player, with no account or login needed.
