Key Features

Screen + Webcam, One Take

Record your display and your camera at the same time — Head Recorder composites them into a single video as you go.

Camera Bubble, Your Way

Drop the camera bubble in any corner and resize it to fit your recording.

Circle or Rectangle

Switch the camera bubble between a circle or rectangle to match your style.

Mirror Your Camera

Mirror the camera feed so it looks natural in the frame, on or off with one toggle.

Pick Your Sources

Choose which display, camera, and microphone to record from — or record with no microphone at all.

Private by Design

No accounts, no servers — recording and processing happen entirely on your Mac.
